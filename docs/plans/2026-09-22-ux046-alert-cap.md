# UX-046: bounded alert capacity priority

## Brief

Goal: when the alert store exceeds its existing 500-item capacity, retain pinned
and unacknowledged alerts ahead of acknowledged unpinned alerts.
Value: the operator's retained and unhandled warnings survive routine alert load.
Base: main 928696b02e3c80bd471318c2b90ae7a41ac117fd.
Risk: high assurance; discovery/design and operator approval completed before
production implementation.

The earlier read-only reproduction showed one pinned and one unacknowledged
alert discarded while all 500 acknowledged alerts survived. Correcting this
ordering is independent of changing alert age or migration policy.

Approved acceptance: remove unpinned acknowledged items first, then unpinned
unacknowledged items, then pinned items only if needed to maintain the hard cap;
oldest first within each approved group. Define deterministic ties and the
all-pinned overflow case. Preserve notification, ack/pin, source timestamp,
flush, archive and hydration behavior outside that capacity operation.

Constraints: no new provider, inference, schema, storage migration, capacity
increase, installed-profile change or real notifications in tests. Existing
age pruning is a separate unresolved problem, not certified by this repair.
Coordinate with open PR1732; do not duplicate or undo its hydration purge.

Discovery and architecture review resolved the policy: three eviction groups,
unpinned acknowledged, unpinned unacknowledged, and all pinned regardless of
acknowledgment. Use ascending source timestamp within each group. Equal timestamps
retain stable Map insertion order, so the earlier insertion is evicted first;
updating an existing ID does not reset its position. If more than 500 entries are
pinned, oldest pinned entries can be evicted. The operator approved this bounded
policy on September 22, 2026 ("do it" in response to the concrete overflow
policy). Draft PR #1734 claims UX-046.

The hard cap applies after existing capacity enforcement, not at every instant:
ordinary ingestion batches may exceed 500 until flush, ingestion above 1,000
also prunes immediately, and historical oversized hydration is unchanged.
Production persist follows prune; the existing serialization backstop is not
being redesigned. PR1732 at d2ede224 touches constants and hydration but not this
capacity comparator. Its purge remains separately owned.

Owners: repository analyst refreshes the discovery; architect designs the
bounded policy; specialist implements after approval and draft claim; independent
reviewer and Claude audit the final diff and evidence.

Validation: public store behavior with in-memory storage and notification/archive
stubs; mixed priorities, timestamp ties, exact capacity, all-pinned overflow and
persist/reload parity. Record test-first failures, applied-diff clean-tree mutation
proofs, type checks, named agentic gate and independent/exact-tip review.
Rollback: reviewed comparator revert; no migration or data cleanup. A revert does
not recover alerts already evicted from live/localStorage state. The existing
best-effort archive is not a guaranteed recovery source.

UX-046 owns the capacity correction. Source-age retention is UX-059, preserving
its acceptance and coordination with NWS lifecycle and PR1732. Do not mark the
broader age problem complete.
