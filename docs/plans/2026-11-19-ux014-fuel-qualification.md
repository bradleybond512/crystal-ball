## Decision and evidence boundary

Keep OSM fuel sites directory-only and all current operational facts unknown.
France's official snapshot has public reuse rights and a verified schema, but
fresh file publication is not recent confirmation of every retained declaration.
Old start dates do not establish that reports are invalid or resolved either.
Do not infer availability from prices, hours or absent shortage reports.

The [qualification report](../validation/UX-014-FUEL-SOURCE-QUALIFICATION.md)
records the complete 9,824-station body, fuel-type counts, timestamp semantics,
rights and unresolved lifecycle requirements. This is qualification evidence,
not an adapter benchmark or installed-app acceptance.

## Remaining requirements for any later adapter

- Establish what active/removed/corrected declarations mean, source completeness,
  per-record confirmation versus event time, raw timestamp timezone and expiry.
- Keep temporary shortage, permanent non-distribution and unknown separate;
  never promote one product's status to station-wide inventory or open status.
- Attribute the publisher and update date accurately under the actual licence;
  preserve publication, report-start and retrieval times as separate facts.
- Design bounded requests/cache/body limits, exact origin allowlists, normalized
  fields and health based on usable adapter output. Reject malformed rows and
  bodies without turning empty/unusable evidence into healthy operational votes.
- Prove withdrawal/expiry to unknown and failure paths with behavioral tests,
  live body evidence and applied mutation proofs. Packaged acceptance stays held.

An age-labelled source notice could describe only a declaration included in a
snapshot while current status remains unknown. That is a separate product/design
decision, not authorization here and not completion of operational evidence.
